Automotive · Performance Machine

Shock Absorber Design and Development

Designed and validated automotive shock absorbers for Toyota Tacoma, Jeep Wrangler, and Jeep Gladiator. Led DFM analysis on tube geometry, IFP materials, spring seat contact areas, and snap ring strength. Collaborated with suppliers on manufacturing and 3D-printed fixtures for laser engraving operations.

EV Heat Exchanger Test Fixture

Designed and assembled a full test environment to validate EV charging station heat exchangers under high-temperature field conditions. Biomedical · Academic

Grasping Prosthetic Device

Designed and built a grasping prosthetic device with natural joint movement. Used SolidWorks Motion Study to simulate joint angles and validate kinematics before fabrication. Iterated through prototype testing and validation, gaining end-to-end design experience from concept to working physical prototype.

Walking Kinematics of 6-Legged Robot

Designed and built a six-legged walking robot with fully articulated leg mechanisms driven by a DC motor system. Performed kinematic analysis of the leg linkage geometry and assembled the preliminary prototype.

Steerable Robot Walker

Designed a steerable walking robot through two full prototype iterations from electromechanical first prototype to a laser-cut second prototype. Applied statics analysis and SolidWorks Motion Study simulation to validate walking mechanism before fabrication and assembly.
